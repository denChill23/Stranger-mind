Stranger mind by den_chill

I'm creating an atmospheric 2D game in Unity, and this is my development blog. Everyone is welcome to check out the demo version of my game, and I welcome your feedback on my social media.

You've landed on an unknown planet and must find a way to escape. You'll be aided by a mysterious stranger who knows things about this planet you don't. You need to use the second dimension to overcome high walls and deep holes.

Link to demo version (ZIP)

You cannot download the game on a phone — use a desktop or laptop browser. The download link is disabled on small screens.

Set demoZipUrl in js/posts.js to enable the download link on desktop.
